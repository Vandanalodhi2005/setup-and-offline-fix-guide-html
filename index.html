Why Is My Printer Offline?

A printer showing offline means your computer can't currently communicate with the printer. It doesn't always mean there's a problem with the printer itself.

7. Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up a new printer?
Turn on your printer, complete the initial hardware setup, choose a connection method, add the printer to your computer, and install any required software from the applicable manufacturer's official website.
Why is my printer showing offline?
Your computer may be unable to communicate with the printer because of a Wi-Fi interruption, incorrect printer settings, a disconnected cable, network differences, or a stuck print job.
How do I fix printer offline issues?
Check that your printer is powered on and connected, confirm your computer and printer are using the appropriate network, verify the correct printer is selected, and clear any stuck print jobs.
Why is my printer not connecting to Wi-Fi?
Check your Wi-Fi connection, network selection, printer settings, router availability, and whether the printer is within an appropriate range of your wireless network.
How do I connect a printer to my computer?
Connect your printer through Wi-Fi, USB, or Ethernet, then open your computer's printer settings and select the option to add a printer.
My printer is connected but not printing. What should I do?
Check the print queue, printer status, paper supply, ink or toner, connection, selected printer, and any required printer software.
Why is my scanner not working?
Scanner problems may result from connection issues, software settings, computer permissions, device detection, or configuration problems.
Where can I download the correct printer drivers?
Download printer-specific drivers and software from the applicable printer manufacturer's official website.
